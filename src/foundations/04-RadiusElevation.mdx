# Radius & Elevation

**Decision:** generous radii (the deck doesn't use near-square corners) and shadow always soft —
low alpha, high blur. No hard shadows or 2-4px radii.

**When to use:** `none` — full-bleed/background image. `sm` — chips, tags, small icon.
`md` — default card (most components). `lg` — large frame (ImageFrame, hero panel).
`pill` — badge, dot, pre-title, anything rounded at the ends.

## Do / Don't

**Do**
- Use `radius-md` as the card default; only go up to `lg` when the element is
  visually "hero" (large frame, highlighted panel).
- Keep shadows soft — always `rgba(5,5,8,.04–.08)`, never solid black.

**Don't**
- Use a square radius (`none`) on a content card — it's only for full-bleed.
- Stack two shadows or use a shadow outside the 3 tokens.

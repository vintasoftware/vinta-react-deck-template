# Modes & Contrast

**Decision:** the deck has 3 semantic modes. Slides are **light by default**; covers and dividers
use **invert**. Of the 24 source screens, 17 were light and 7 inverted — invert is the exception, not the
rule. Contrast targets **AA** on all text (the deck goes on a projector, not close-up reading).

## Core guardrail: an accent never carries a title

An accent (any of the 5 secondaries) is a **detail/marker** — tick, underline, label,
card tint. It is never the color of a title or of a block of running text. Text emphasis
on a colored surface is always **white**, never an accent.

`cyan` is the strictest case: it fails contrast on a light background (~1.4:1) and on blue itself
(~1.9:1, same color family) — it only works as text on `invert-navy`. In those two cases the
accent system already resolves to white automatically (see tokens.css).

## Do / Don't

**Do**
- Light by default; reserve invert for cover/divider/transition moment.
- Pick the mode by semantic role (`data-mode`), never apply a loose color.
- Always target AA — the projector washes out contrast, weak text disappears.

**Don't**
- Use an accent as a title color.
- Use `cyan` as text outside `invert-navy`.
- Mix two modes in the same content block (e.g. light text inside an invert card).

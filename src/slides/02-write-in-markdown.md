# Or write a slide in markdown

## Pick a layout
Add src/slides/NN-name.md with
template: NN in its frontmatter.

## Write the content
"#" is the title; "##" sections
become cards, items or stats.

## Fine-tune
Anything the body can't say
goes under props: up top.

## Mix freely
.md and .tsx slides share one
deck, ordered by filename.
